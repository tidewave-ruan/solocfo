常见问题

我的账目数据存在哪里？会上传吗？
全部保存在你自己的设备上，并已加密。本应用没有服务器、没有账号，数据不会上传，我们也看不到。详见隐私政策。
忘记密码怎么办？
用你在首次设置时抄下的 16 位恢复码重设密码。恢复码只展示那一次，不会上传，我们没有副本——若密码和恢复码同时遗失，任何人都无法解开账本。请务必把恢复码抄在安全的地方。
换手机了，数据怎么带过去？
在旧设备用「导出加密备份」生成备份文件，转移到新设备后用「从备份恢复」导入。备份文件全程加密，用你的密码或恢复码都能打开。
怎么彻底删除我的数据？
应用内可以删除账户（该账户下没有流水时才删得掉）。要清空全部数据，直接删除本应用即可——iOS 会一并删除它在设备上的所有数据，包括加密账本与应用文件夹内的备份文件。你此前另存到别处的备份副本不在其中，需要你自行删除。
这个 App 需要什么权限？
不需要任何系统权限。它只读写属于自己的应用沙盒目录，不访问相机、照片、位置或通讯录。

Frequently asked questions

Where is my financial data stored? Is it uploaded?
Everything stays encrypted on your own device. The app has no server and no accounts, so your data is never uploaded and we cannot see it. See our Privacy Policy.
I forgot my password.
Use the 16-character recovery code you wrote down during setup to reset your password. The code is shown only once, is never uploaded, and we keep no copy — if you lose both your password and your recovery code, no one can decrypt your ledger. Please keep it somewhere safe.
How do I move my data to a new phone?
On the old device, use "Export encrypted backup". Transfer the file to the new device and use "Restore from backup". The backup stays encrypted throughout and opens with either your password or your recovery code.
How do I delete all my data?
You can delete an account in-app (only while it has no transactions). To erase everything, simply delete the app — iOS removes all of its on-device data, including the encrypted ledger and any backup files inside the app's own folder. Backup copies you saved elsewhere must be deleted by you.
What permissions does the app need?
None. It only reads and writes its own application sandbox, and does not access your camera, photos, location or contacts.
