隐私政策

适用于 掌柜CFO（中国区）／ Solo CFO（国际区）iOS 应用
生效日期：2026 年 8 月 5 日 ｜ 最后更新：2026 年 8 月 12 日

一句话概括：本应用不收集你的任何个人数据。你的全部账目数据以加密形式保存在你自己的设备上，从不上传、也无处可传——本应用没有服务器、没有账号体系。

1. 我们收集哪些数据

我们不收集任何数据。具体而言：

  • 本应用没有用户账号、没有注册登录，我们不知道也无从知道你是谁。
  • 你录入的账户、流水、金额、备注等全部财务信息，仅保存在你设备的本地存储中，并以 AES-256-GCM 加密后存放。解密所需的密钥由你设置的密码与恢复码派生，我们不持有、也无法获取。
  • 本应用不含任何第三方分析、统计、广告或崩溃上报 SDK。
  • 我们不收集设备标识符、位置、通讯录、日历、健康数据或任何其他个人信息。

2. 系统权限

本应用不请求任何系统权限——不访问相机、照片、位置、通讯录、麦克风、日历或健康数据。

本应用只读写属于它自己的应用沙盒目录（用于保存你的加密账本与备份文件），此操作无需向你索取额外授权，其他应用也无法读取该目录。

3. 数据的用途

你的数据只有一个用途：在你自己的设备上，为你计算和展示你的账目、负债、利息与现金流。

我们不将其用于任何其他目的，不做用户画像，不做定向广告，不作训练数据，也不以任何形式对外提供。

4. 第三方

本应用不与任何第三方共享用户数据——因为我们本身就不持有这些数据。本应用不集成第三方分析、广告网络、崩溃上报或数据代理服务。

本应用当前不含应用内购买。若今后加入，相关交易将由 Apple 处理并适用 Apple 的隐私政策，我们仍不会获得你的支付方式或账单信息，届时也会先行更新本政策。

5. 数据留存与删除

  • 留存：你的数据保存在你设备本地，保存多久完全由你决定。我们服务器上没有你的任何数据副本——因为我们没有服务器。
  • 删除：你可以在应用内删除账户（该账户下没有流水时才删得掉）。要清空全部数据，删除本应用即可——iOS 会一并删除它在设备上的所有数据，包括加密账本与应用文件夹内的备份文件。你此前另存到其他位置或已发送出去的备份副本不在其中，需要你自行删除。
  • 撤回同意：本应用不请求任何系统权限，因此没有需要撤回的授权；你可随时删除本应用，其全部数据随之删除。
  • 向我们请求删除：由于我们从未持有你的数据，无需也无法代你执行删除。删除权完全在你手上。

6. 数据安全

本地账本采用 AES-256-GCM 信封加密。密钥由你的密码派生，并可由你的 16 位恢复码解开。恢复码只在设置时向你展示一次，不会上传、我们也不留存副本——因此若密码与恢复码同时遗失，任何人（包括我们）都无法解开你的账本。请务必妥善保管恢复码。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其任何信息（事实上本应用不收集任何人的信息）。

8. 政策变更

若本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面顶部的「最后更新」日期。重大变更将在应用内一并告知。

9. 联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，请联系：tidewave@163.com

Privacy Policy

For 掌柜CFO (China) / Solo CFO (International) iOS app
Effective: August 5, 2026 ｜ Last updated: August 12, 2026

In one sentence: This app collects no personal data. All of your financial data stays encrypted on your own device. It is never uploaded — and there is nowhere to upload it to: this app has no server and no user accounts.

1. What data we collect

We collect nothing. Specifically:

  • The app has no user accounts and no sign-up or sign-in. We do not know, and cannot know, who you are.
  • Every account, transaction, amount and note you enter is stored solely in local storage on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The decryption key is derived from the password and recovery code that you choose; we neither hold nor can obtain it.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, calendars, health data, or any other personal information.

2. System permissions

This app requests no system permissions — no camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone, calendar or health data.

It only reads and writes within its own application sandbox (to store your encrypted ledger and backup files). This requires no additional permission from you, and no other app can read that directory.

3. How your data is used

Your data serves exactly one purpose: to calculate and display your balances, debts, interest and cash flow — on your own device.

We do not use it for anything else. No profiling, no targeted advertising, no training data, and no disclosure to anyone.

4. Third parties

We share no user data with third parties — we do not hold any to share. The app integrates no third-party analytics, ad networks, crash reporting or data brokers.

The app currently offers no in-app purchases. Should that change, such transactions would be handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we still would not receive your payment method or billing details, and we would update this policy beforehand.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Retention: Your data lives on your device for exactly as long as you choose. There is no copy on our servers, because we have no servers.
  • Deletion: You can delete an account in-app (only while it has no transactions). To erase everything, simply delete the app — iOS removes all of its on-device data, including the encrypted ledger and any backup files inside the app's own folder. Backup copies you previously saved elsewhere or sent to someone are not covered and must be deleted by you.
  • Withdrawing consent: The app requests no system permissions, so there is nothing to revoke. You may delete the app at any time, which removes all of its data.
  • Requesting deletion from us: Since we have never held your data, there is nothing for us to delete and no request is needed. Deletion is entirely in your hands.

6. Security

Your local ledger uses AES-256-GCM envelope encryption. The key is derived from your password and can also be unlocked by your 16-character recovery code. The recovery code is shown to you once during setup; it is never uploaded and we keep no copy. If you lose both your password and your recovery code, no one — including us — can decrypt your ledger. Please store your recovery code safely.

7. Children's privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them (indeed, we collect information from no one).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be announced in-app.
